Core Capabilities

Everything a clinical team needs, remotely.

From bedside readings to doctor review — RGMS keeps the care loop closed without the patient leaving home.

01 — Caregiver Entry
📋

Simple, Guided Vitals Entry

Caregivers use a clean, guided form to log blood pressure, glucose, pulse rate, symptoms, and free-text notes — from any device, any time.

02 — Automated Flagging
🚨

Critical Alert Triage

Abnormal readings are automatically flagged so no dangerous value slips through unnoticed.

BP 182/110 — Critical high 2m ago
Glucose 14.2 — Elevated 14m ago
Pulse 88 bpm — Normal range 1h ago
03 — Doctor Oversight
🏥

Clinician Command Center

Doctors review urgent cases, add clinical notes, and mark alerts as resolved — all from a single dashboard.

04 — Patient History
📈

Longitudinal Records

Every submission is stored with patient identity, timestamp, and caregiver attribution — a complete, auditable health trail.

05 — Live Monitoring
⚡

Real-Time Updates

Readings surface to the clinical team the moment they're submitted. No delays, no batch processing.

Workflow

Three steps. One closed care loop.

RGMS was designed to be fast to adopt and simple to use — for caregivers and clinicians alike.

STEP 01
📝

Caregiver Submits

The caregiver enters the patient's vitals into a simple, guided form — blood pressure, glucose, pulse, symptoms, and any notes.

STEP 02
🔔

System Flags Abnormals

RGMS automatically analyses each submission against clinical thresholds and flags critical or elevated readings in real time.

STEP 03
🩺

Doctor Reviews & Acts

The physician sees flagged alerts on their dashboard, reviews the patient's history, and marks cases as reviewed or escalates action.
