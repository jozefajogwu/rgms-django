Core Features

Everything your care team needs, remotely.

From bedside readings to doctor review — RGMS keeps the care loop closed without the patient leaving home.

📋

Caregiver Entry

Simple, guided form for BP, blood sugar, pulse rate, symptoms, and free-text notes — from any device, any time.

🚨

Critical Alerts

Abnormal readings are automatically flagged so doctors can triage and act without delay — no reading slips through.

🏥

Doctor Oversight

Clinicians review urgent cases, add clinical notes, and mark alerts as resolved — all from a single dashboard.

📈

Patient History

Every reading is stored with patient details, caregiver ID, and timestamp — a complete, auditable health trail.

Workflow

Three steps. One closed care loop.

Designed to be fast to adopt and simple to use — for caregivers and clinicians alike.

1

Caregiver Submits

The caregiver enters the patient's vitals — blood pressure, glucose, pulse, symptoms, and notes — into a simple guided form.

2

System Flags Abnormals

RGMS analyses each submission against clinical thresholds and instantly flags critical or elevated readings for review.

3

Doctor Reviews & Acts

The physician sees flagged alerts on their dashboard, reviews the patient's history, and marks cases as reviewed or escalates.

Live Monitoring

Abnormal readings surface instantly.

The moment a caregiver submits a critical value, it appears on the doctor's dashboard — colour-coded by severity, with full patient context alongside it.
